\subsection{The Dedekind--Ramanujan Bridge: Time-Reversal Invariance}
\label{sec:dedekind-ramanujan}
% ================================================================

The off-diagonal Gram entries decompose into Ramanujan terms (controlled
by $\gcd(j,k)^2/(12jk)$) and Dedekind sum corrections. The Cathedral
formalizes this connection through a \textbf{three-file architecture}
that resolves a circular dependency while establishing the full
reciprocity law.

\subsubsection{The Dedekind Sum}

The Dedekind sum $s(b,a)$ for coprime positive integers $a, b$ is defined via
the sawtooth function $((x)) = x - \lfloor x \rfloor - 1/2$ (for $x \notin \ZZ$):
\[
  s(b,a) = \sum_{m=1}^{a-1} \left(\!\left(\frac{m}{a}\right)\!\right)
  \left(\!\left(\frac{mb}{a}\right)\!\right)
\]

\begin{correspondence}[Dedekind Reciprocity as Time-Reversal]
\label{corr:dedekind-reciprocity}
The Dedekind reciprocity law
\[
  s(a,b) + s(b,a) = \frac{a^2 + b^2 + 1}{12ab} - \frac{1}{4}
\]
is the number-theoretic analogue of \emph{time-reversal invariance}
of the S-matrix on the prime lattice. The scattering amplitude
$V(a,b) + V(b,a)$ (Vasyunin cotangent sum pair) decomposes via
Dedekind sums, and the reciprocity law constrains this sum to
a universal kinematic factor depending only on $a^2 + b^2$.

In quantum field theory, time-reversal invariance (T-symmetry)
constrains the S-matrix: $S(a \to b) = S(b \to a)$ up to
kinematic factors. The Dedekind reciprocity law is the exact
analogue, relating the forward scattering $s(a,b)$ to the
backward scattering $s(b,a)$ via a symmetric correction.
\end{correspondence}

\subsubsection{The Three-File Architecture}

The formalization employs a three-file decomposition to resolve
a circular dependency:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \lean{DedekindReciprocity.lean}: Self-contained definitions

  \item \lean{DedekindBridge.lean}: The ``Brave Berry'' --- a weighted

The architecture mirrors the physical decomposition: the S-matrix

\subsubsection{The Ramanujan Connection}

The Ramanujan entry $R(j,k) = \gcd(j,k)^2/(12jk)$ appears as the
leading term in the Dedekind reciprocity expansion:
\[
  s(a,b) + s(b,a) = R(a,b) + \frac{a^2 + b^2}{12ab} - \frac{1}{4}
\]
(where $R(a,b) = 1/(12ab)$ for coprime $a,b$).

The Ramanujan matrix $R$ encodes the \emph{tree-level}
interaction between modes $j$ and $k$ in the prime number quantum field.
The Dedekind correction $s(a,b) + s(b,a) - R(a,b)$ is the
\emph{one-loop correction}: it captures the modular arithmetic of
the coprime structure that goes beyond the simple GCD scaling.

This decomposition is proved in the Cathedral with zero sorry
in the core infrastructure (\lean{DedekindAssembly.lean},
\lean{RamanujanBridge.lean}), establishing a rigorous bridge
between the Vasyunin scattering amplitudes and the Ramanujan
quadratic form that controls the NB distance.
