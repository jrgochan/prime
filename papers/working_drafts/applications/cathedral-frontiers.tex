\begin{abstract}
We survey the open problems and research directions suggested by
the Cathedral project, spanning mathematics, physics, engineering,
medicine, AI collaboration, and formalization methodology.
The Cathedral reduced the Riemann Hypothesis to one axiom
and discovered a structural correspondence between the integers
and gauge field theory captured by five Pentagon insights.
We identify the mathematical infrastructure needed to close the
remaining axioms, the physical questions raised by the Torus
Projection on $T^\infty = \prod_p S^1_p$, the medical applications
of the Ward Health Index, the broader implications of the
human-AI pair programming model, and a vision for verified
knowledge beyond mathematics.
\end{abstract}

\section{Closing the One Crown Axiom}
% ================================================================

The sole crown axiom is \lean{overcancellation\_axiom}, formally
proved \emph{equivalent} to the Riemann Hypothesis via the Glass Box
architecture. The two PNT axioms on the cleanest path are
unconditional consequences of the Prime Number Theorem.
Closing the crown requires connecting the existing infrastructure
(see BOUNTY.md for the detailed gap analysis):

\begin{enumerate}
  \item The Littlewood Maneuver (\textbf{PROVED}, 0 axioms).
  \item The Parseval Bridge (\textbf{PROVED}, 0 axioms).
  \item The Perron chain giving $M(x) = O(x^{1/2+\varepsilon})$
    under RH (\textbf{PROVED}, 0 axioms).
  \item The truncation error bound for Dirichlet polynomials
    (\textbf{GAP}: 500--800 lines, 2--3 weeks).
  \item The Kronecker/Bohr approximation theorem
    (\textbf{GAP}: 300--500 lines, 1--2 weeks).
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Estimated total}: 900--1{,}500 lines, 3--6 weeks for an
expert team. 95\% of the infrastructure already exists.

\subsection{Alternative Paths}

Three alternative forward paths provide cross-validation:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Mellin Crown} (2 axioms): Via the Hardy--Littlewood

  \item \textbf{Perron Crown} (4 axioms): Via real-variable Mertens

Closing any alternative-path axiom strengthens the multi-path

\subsection{The Oracle Bridge (Path D)}

The v17 Oracle Capstone introduces a fourth path: the
\emph{compute oracle}. The full proof chain from trusted computation
to RH is already proved:
\begin{center}
\small
\texttt{oracle\_certificates} (trusted GPU) $\to$
\texttt{gram\_subseq\_from\_certificates} (proved) $\to$
\texttt{rh\_from\_certificates} (proved) $\to$ RH
\end{center}
The sole axiom is \lean{oracle\_certificates}: the assertion that
DD-precision computation of $v^T G v$ at highly composite numbers
yields the stated bounds. This follows the Flyspeck precedent
(trusted computation backing formal proof).

\textbf{Open problem}: Graduate \lean{oracle\_certificates} from
axiom to theorem by kernel-verifying $v^T G v < 1$ at even one $N$.
The blocker is that \lean{logCutoffWitness} uses \lean{Real.log},
which is noncomputable in Lean~4. Three approaches exist (rational
bounding, \lean{native\_decide} with computable proxy, interval
arithmetic), each requiring 500--1{,}000 lines. This is a natural
community contribution target.

\subsection{The Orthogonality Shield (Path E)}

The spectral observatory (Exploration 33) discovered that the
decoupling exponent $\beta(N) > 1$ for all measured $N$, with
$\beta \approx 0.18 \ln N$. If $\beta > 1$ can be proved
\emph{unconditionally} (without assuming RH), it would provide
a real-spectral proof that $d_N^2 \to 0$, bypassing the
complex-analytic machinery entirely. This is a deep open problem
connecting Gram matrix spectral theory to the Heisenberg picture
of arithmetic.

\section{Algorithmic Questions}
% ================================================================

\subsection{Fast Gram Matrix Computation}

Computing $G_N$ exactly via Vasyunin's formula costs $O(N^2 \log N)$.
Can this be improved? The Fourier structure of the cotangent sums
suggests an $O(N \log^2 N)$ algorithm via FFT.

\subsection{The Eigenvalue Distribution}

Numerical experiments at f64 precision suggested
$\lambda_{\min}(G_N) \sim N^{-2}$, but DD-precision (MPFR-256)
measurements show $\lambda_{\min} \approx 2.5 \times 10^{-7}$ at
$N = 10{,}000$ (24$\times$ larger than f64), consistent with
logarithmic scaling $\lambda_{\min} \sim c/\ln N$. The condition
number $\kappa(G_N) > 10^7$ at these scales defeats IEEE~754
double precision.
$\lambda_{\max}(G_N) \sim \log N$ is robust across precisions.

\textbf{Open problem}: Prove the true asymptotic scaling of
$\lambda_{\min}(G_N)$. The connection to random matrix theory
(GOE spacing statistics) suggests tools from integrable probability.

\subsection{Optimal Weight Computation}

The optimal BD weights $v = G^{-1}b$ grow like $\|v\|^2 = \Theta(N)$
while $d_N^2 \to 0$. The Sherman--Morrison rank-1 update
$G_{N+1} = G_N + uu^T$ enables $O(N^2)$ online updating. Can this
be reduced further?

\subsection{The Spectral Universality Conjecture}

Exploration 19 established experimentally that the spectral
statistics of the BD Gram matrix transition from Poisson (small $N$)
to GOE (large $N$) at a critical threshold
$N_c \approx 60 \cdot m/\varphi(m)$.

\textbf{Open problem}: Prove the universality of this threshold.
This would connect the Cathedral to the Bohigas--Giannoni--Schmit
conjecture in quantum chaos.

\subsection{The $\alpha \approx 0.47$ Constant}

The participation ratio stabilizes at $\alpha \approx 0.47$,
between full localization and GOE delocalization.

\textbf{Open problem}: Derive $\alpha$ from the zeta function.
This may involve the pair correlation of zeta zeros
(Montgomery's conjecture).

\subsection{Generalized Nyman--Beurling}

The Cathedral uses $h_k(x) = \{1/(kx)\}$. What happens with:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $h_k(x) = \{k^\alpha/x\}$ for $\alpha \neq 1$?
  \item $h_k(x) = \{1/(kx)\} \cdot w(x)$ for weights $w$?
  \item Analogues for Dirichlet $L$-functions?
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The M\"obius Stratum Convergence Conjecture}

The GCD Partition theorem (\texttt{Covariance/GCDPartition.lean},
zero sorry) decomposes the BD quadratic form as
$d_N^2 = \sum_{d \mid N!} S_d$.
GPU experiments at $N = 55{,}440$ reveal an 88\% correlation between
$\operatorname{sgn}(S_d)$ and $\mu(d)$.

\textbf{Conjecture (M\"obius Stratum Convergence)}: For each
squarefree $d$, $\operatorname{sgn}(S_d) = \mu(d)$ for all
sufficiently large $N$, and the net cancellation
$\sum_d S_d \to 0$ at rate $O(1/\ln N)$.

Proving this conjecture would give a \emph{purely arithmetic} proof
of $d_N^2 \to 0$, with the cancellation emerging from the
M\"obius function's sign structure on the divisor lattice rather
than from complex analysis.

\subsection{Certified Computation at Scale}

The DD-precision pipeline (Dekker--Knuth Double-Double arithmetic)
has extended Gram matrix certification to $N = 55{,}440$. Scaling
further requires:
\begin{itemize}
  \item GPU-accelerated DD matvec for $N > 100{,}000$.
  \item Out-of-core Gram matrix construction for systems exceeding
    RAM capacity.
  \item Streaming DD-precision CG with checkpoint/restart.
  \item Parallel certification across moduli.
\end{itemize}

The Dekker--Knuth boundary ($N \approx 40{,}000$) marks the
transition from f64-tractable to DD-required computation. Beyond
$N \approx 200{,}000$, even DD arithmetic may exhaust precision,
requiring triple-double or MPFR-backed CG solvers.

\subsection{The Glass Box Methodology (v24)}

The Glass Box architecture (v24) decomposed the single opaque
\lean{overcancellation\_axiom} into 7 transparent sub-axioms,
then graduated 5 of them to theorems. This \emph{axiom
decomposition} methodology is transferable to any formalization
project with opaque assumptions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Identify the opaque axiom.
  \item Decompose it into elementary sub-claims.
  \item Graduate the elementary sub-claims one by one.
  \item The irreducible residual reveals the \emph{true} unknown
    content.
\end{enumerate}
This is the formalization analogue of a physics experiment
isolating a signal from background noise.

\subsection{The Mellin Bridge and Hi-Lo Crossover (v26)}

The Hi-Lo decomposition (\texttt{MellinBridge/HiLoDecomposition.lean})
splits the Mellin integral at a crossover frequency $T_0(N)$,
routing the low-frequency bulk through PNT estimates and the
high-frequency tail through oscillation bounds. This
\emph{crossover technique} is applicable wherever an integral
or sum has qualitatively different behavior in different regimes
---a common pattern in signal processing, statistical physics,
and numerical analysis.

\subsection{The Arithmetic Standard Model (v25--v26)}

The 101-theorem Arithmetic Standard Model
(\texttt{Physics/StandardModel/}) establishes a complete dictionary

  \item \textbf{Confinement}: The formalized confinement theorem

\section{Conclusion}

The Cathedral is an ending and a beginning. The proof architecture
is complete; the questions it raises are not. The research frontier
extends in at least eight directions: closing the crown axiom,
understanding the spectral universality, exploiting the algorithmic
structure (including the DD-precision pipeline), contributing to
Mathlib, generalizing to other $L$-functions, testing the Pentagon
insights in biology and physics, deploying the Ward Health Index
in medicine, and building the next generation of human-AI
proof engineering collaborations.

The mathematics does not choose sides. But by making it verified,
open, and accessible, we ensure it serves the light.

\subsection{The Ward Health Index}

The Ward Health Index (WHI), defined as
$\text{WHI}(M, \pi) = 1 - |W|/\operatorname{tr}(M)$
for a symmetric matrix $M$ with binary partition $\pi$, provides
a single-scalar diagnostic for parity-structured systems.

\textbf{Medical frontiers}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Stroke detection}: Hemispheric EEG coherence with
    left/right parity. WHI~$\approx 1$ in healthy subjects; WHI~$\ll 1$
    in unilateral stroke. Testable against public EEG datasets
    (Temple University Hospital corpus).
  \item \textbf{Cardiac monitoring}: Left/right bundle branch ECG
    parity. WHI as a bedside alarm for bundle branch blocks.
  \item \textbf{Gene co-expression}: Chromosomal parity of gene
    expression panels for cancer screening.
\end{itemize}

constant of the integers. Biologically:
