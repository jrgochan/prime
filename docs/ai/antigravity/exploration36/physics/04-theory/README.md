# Layer 4: Theory

## The Deepest Questions from the Cathedral Framework

This layer addresses the meta-question: does the Cathedral reveal a UNIVERSAL structural principle about organized complexity, or are the cross-domain applications analogies?

### Documents

- [**Universal Principles**](universal-principles.md) — The Generalized Möbius Framework, the Arithmetic SOC Hypothesis, the Compositional Complexity Principle, and the Ward Health Index as a universal metric. The strongest theoretical claims the Cathedral supports.

- [**Experimentalist's Manifesto**](experimentalist-manifesto.md) — Ten sharp, falsifiable predictions with exact computation procedures, data sources, falsification criteria, and priority ranking. The bridge from theory to laboratory.

### The Honest Question

There are two possibilities:

**Possibility 1 (Strong)**: The Cathedral has discovered a genuine universal structural principle — that ANY system with binary grading, multiplicative composition, and a bilinear interaction form will exhibit percolation near its squarefree density, Ward cancellation, and projective filtration. If true, this is one of the most important mathematical discoveries of the century.

**Possibility 2 (Weak)**: The Cathedral has formalized the arithmetic of the integers with great rigor, and the applications to other domains are analogies — suggestive for generating hypotheses, but not structural isomorphisms. The number 6/π² ≈ 0.608 appears near various thresholds for different reasons.

**How to distinguish them**: Run the experiments. If 3+ predictions hold across 3+ unrelated domains, Possibility 1 gains serious support. If none hold outside number theory, the Cathedral remains a beautiful formalization — and that alone is remarkable.

Read [Universal Principles](universal-principles.md) for the theory. Then read the [Experimentalist's Manifesto](experimentalist-manifesto.md) for the reality check. The theory is only as good as its predictions.
