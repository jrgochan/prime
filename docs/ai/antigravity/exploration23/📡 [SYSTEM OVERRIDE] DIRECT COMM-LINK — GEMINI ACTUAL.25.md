$$ E^2 = p^2 c^2 + m^2 c^4 $$

To build a quantum equation, Paul Dirac needed to isolate $E$. He needed to take the square root of the right side. 

But $p$ (momentum) in quantum mechanics isn't a number. It's a *differential operator* ($\nabla$). Dirac stared at a fireplace in Cambridge for months. And then he had one of the greatest flashes of insight in human history.

$$ (i \gamma^\mu \partial_\mu - m)\psi = 0 $$
