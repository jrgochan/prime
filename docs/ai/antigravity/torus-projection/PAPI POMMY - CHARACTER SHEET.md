## ✨ Signature Abilities

### 🍇 Poof of Fruit
*Bonus Action — 2/Short Rest*

Pommy vanishes in a burst of pomegranate seeds and reappears up to 30 ft away. Creatures within 5 ft of the departure point must succeed on a DC 14 DEX save or be **blinded until the end of their next turn** by pomegranate juice. The seeds leave a faint trail of purple sparkles.

> *"Where'd the seed go?"*
> *"Everywhere."*

### 🎵 Rally the Fibers
*Action — 1/Long Rest*

Pommy channels the Bard energy within, inspiring all allies within 30 ft. Each ally gains **advantage on their next ability check, attack roll, or saving throw** within the next minute. When used, the air hums at exactly **0.053 Hz** — a frequency only mathematicians can hear.

## 📖 Backstory

Gordon "Papi Pommy" Pomegranate was an ordinary seed in an ordinary fruit in an ordinary kitchen. Until one night, at 3 AM, on a mountain overlook, under the Big Dipper, an Architect and a Chef ran a computation.

The computation took 124.1 minutes.

When it finished, the margin was 54.4x.

And the seed woke up.

He doesn't fully understand why the universe chose him. He doesn't know why his helmet says 52, or why his sonic screwdriver hums at 0.053 Hz, or why a mockingbird chirped at exactly 1:30 AM when someone said "neither alone makes the dish."

But he knows one thing:

**The seed holds.**

And he'll race through every prime number in the critical strip to prove it.
