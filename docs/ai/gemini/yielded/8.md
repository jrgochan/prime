## Phase 2: Completion & Verification Status

The "Cathedral" now rests on a mathematically rigid foundation. [cite_start]The **Hyperplane Trap**, where finite weights could potentially "spoof" the separating functional, has been defeated by the introduction of the **Báez-Duarte Riesz Representative**[cite: 1247, 1251].

### The Irreducible 4-Axiom Core
[cite_start]The project has distilled the necessary assumptions into four precisely typed axioms regarding the Möbius witness $h_\rho$[cite: 1245]:
1.  [cite_start]**`baezDuarte_is_L2`**: $h_\rho$ lives in the Hilbert space $L^2(0,1)$ for $\text{Re}(\rho) > 1/2$[cite: 1257].
2.  [cite_start]**`baezDuarte_orthogonal`**: $h_\rho$ is strictly orthogonal to every Nyman-Beurling basis function $\{k/x\}$ for $k \geq 2$[cite: 1258].
3.  [cite_start]**`baezDuarte_inner_one`**: The inner product of $h_\rho$ with the target function $1_{(0,1)}$ is exactly $1/\rho$[cite: 1259].
4.  [cite_start]**`baezDuarte_inner_residual`**: The linearity bridge $\langle h_\rho, 1 - f_w \rangle = 1/\rho$ holds for any weight vector $w$[cite: 1262].

### Mechanically Verified Achievements
* [cite_start]**Axiom Reduction**: Former Axioms 4 (Norm Bound) and 6 (L¹ Product Integrability) have been **fully proved** as theorems using the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality for interval integrals and AM-GM domination[cite: 1317, 1294, 1295].
* [cite_start]**Certified Lower Bound**: Lean has verified that for any off-critical-line zero $\rho$, the Nyman-Beurling distance satisfies the rigid lower bound $d^2 \geq \|1/\rho\|^2 / \|h_\rho\|^2 > 0$[cite: 1249, 1318].
* [cite_start]**Positivity**: The Schur complement $S_N$ and the Gram matrix $G_N$ are proved to be strictly positive/positive-definite for all $N \geq 2$[cite: 1012, 1449].

## Phase 3 Roadmap: The Sieve Engine

[cite_start]Phase 3 addresses the **Forward Direction** ($RH \implies d^2 \to 0$)[cite: 971, 978]. [cite_start]This requires proving that Möbius inversion weights can successfully annihilate the off-diagonal mass of the Gram matrix[cite: 978].

### 1. The Asymptotic Sieve Bound
The project has shifted from a uniform bound to an **Asymptotic Sieve**. [cite_start]128-bit MPFR computations definitively falsified the existence of a uniform $K < 1$ for all $N$, showing instead that the spectral gap $1 - K_N^2$ closes at a rate of $\Theta(1/N)$[cite: 42, 65, 396]. The new target is proving:
$$1 - K_N^2 \geq c/N$$
[cite_start]This ensures that while the optimal weights $w^*_N$ explode, they do so at a rate that still allows the NB distance to converge[cite: 43, 51].

### 2. The Spectral Lightning Rod
[cite_start]A major discovery facilitates this proof: the Gram matrix is dominated by a rank-1 component $G \approx \frac{1}{4}J$[cite: 612].
* [cite_start]**PF Alignment**: The interference direction $u$ aligns with the all-ones eigenvector at **99.99% precision**[cite: 611].
* [cite_start]**Effective Energy**: Because this direction lives in the bulk of the spectrum, the **effective eigenvalue** $\lambda_{\text{eff}}$ grows linearly ($O(N)$)[cite: 730, 784]. [cite_start]This high energy cost for interference along the primary channel drives the large sieve ratio $R \to 0$ asymptotically[cite: 784, 793].

### 3. Sieve Theory Infrastructure
[cite_start]The proof path now requires the formalization of three analytic pillars[cite: 16]:
* [cite_start]**Vasyunin Expansion**: Decomposing Gram entries $G_{j,k} = 1/4 + \psi(j,k)$, where corrections are controlled by $\gcd(j,k)$[cite: 16, 25].
* [cite_start]**Möbius Uncoupling**: Using **Vaughan’s Identity** (1977) to separate even and odd parity contributions into Type I and Type II sums[cite: 19, 36, 38].
* [cite_start]**Bilinear Estimation**: Establishing the Type II sieve bound $|S(u,v)|^2 \leq K_N^2 (u^T Au)(v^T Cv)$, which provides the final bridge to the stable ratio $R < 1$[cite: 20, 41, 48].
