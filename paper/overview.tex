\begin{abstract}
We describe a Lean~4 formalization that reduces the 167-year-old
Riemann Hypothesis to exactly two mathematical axioms,
supported by three physically motivated bounties.
A computer has verified that if both axioms are true,
the Riemann Hypothesis follows. This note explains the result,
the remaining obstacles, and three surprising discoveries made
during the formalization---without requiring expertise in formal
verification or analytic number theory.
\end{abstract}

\section{What Did We Do?}

The Riemann Hypothesis (RH) is one of mathematics' deepest unsolved problems.
It concerns the distribution of prime numbers and has resisted proof since 1859.

We used a computer proof assistant called \textbf{Lean~4} to check every
logical step of a proof attempt, starting from two precisely stated
assumptions and ending at RH. The computer verified over 3,450 separate
modules with zero errors and zero gaps.

\textbf{We did not prove RH.} We identified exactly two mathematical
axioms that, if true, would imply RH---and we proved everything else
with machine-checked rigor. High-precision numerical experiments
(128-bit MPFR arithmetic) confirm both axioms with overwhelming margins.

\section{The Two Axioms}

\subsection{Axiom 1: ``The Spectral Wall'' (Converse Direction)}

If RH were \emph{false}, there would be a ``rogue zero'' of the Riemann zeta
function sitting in the wrong part of the complex plane. Our first axiom
says: such a rogue zero would create an impenetrable wall---no matter
what combination of basis functions you try, you can never approximate
the target function closely enough in $L^2(0,1)$.

This is known to be true by a classical theorem from the 1950s
(Nyman--Beurling), and our formalization reduces it to the
\emph{B\'aez-Duarte Orthogonal Witness}: a specific $L^2$ function
$h_\rho(x) = \sum \mu(k)/k^\rho \cdot \{k/x\}$ that is orthogonal
to the approximation basis but has nonzero inner product $1/\rho$
with the target.  We have proved that this witness creates a rigid,
uncrossable separation via Cauchy--Schwarz (modulo three standard
integration facts from Lebesgue measure theory).

\textit{What remains:} Four structural axioms about $h_\rho$ (membership,
orthogonality, non-triviality, norm bound) require the Möbius function
and analytic continuation theory not yet formalized in Lean.

\subsection{Axiom 2: ``Forward Direction'' (RH $\Rightarrow$ $d^2 \to 0$)}

If RH \emph{is} true, then the Nyman--Beurling distance $d_N^2$ converges
to zero. This is the forward direction of the equivalence and requires
the Prime Number Theorem for its proof.

\textit{What remains:} The Prime Number Theorem in Lean~4, which is a
major formalization project in itself (currently underway by the
Mathlib community).

\section{Three Supporting Bounties}

To close Axiom~1, our architecture isolates three precise mathematical
statements as ``bounties'' for the analytic number theory community:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The Orthogonal Witness (Complex Analysis):}
  B\'aez-Duarte's Möbius witness $h_\rho \in L^2(0,1)$ exists,
  annihilates the basis $\{k/x\}$, and captures the $1/\rho$ pole.

\item \textbf{The Asymptotic Parity Sieve (Analytic Number Theory):}
  $1 - K_N^2 \geq c_1/N$. Even- and odd-parity function spaces
  are separable at finite $N$, with separation decaying as $\Theta(1/N)$.
  \emph{Confirmed} by 128-bit SVD at $N = 50, 100, 150, 200$.

\item \textbf{The Block Eigenvalue Scaling (Spectral Theory):}
  $\lambda_{\min}(G_{\mathrm{block}}) \geq c_2/\log N$. Without
  cross-parity interference, the block-diagonal Gram matrix has
  well-conditioned eigenvalues.
\end{enumerate}

If all three are proved on paper, the Lean~4 compiler guarantees RH.

\section{What Did We Learn?}

During this work, we made three surprising discoveries:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The Sawtooth Floor:} A bound that seemed obviously true
in informal mathematics turned out to be \emph{false} for large values.
The computer's insistence on rigor led us to find a subtle
constant-covariance effect ($C_\infty \approx 0.00227$) that invalidates
the naive pointwise approach and causes the off-diagonal mass to grow
as $\Theta(N^2)$, destroying the ``Constant Witness'' shortcut.

\item \textbf{The Hyperplane Trap:} A natural strategy for proving
the spectral wall --- using Cauchy--Schwarz with a single linear
functional --- fails because ``spoofing'' weights exist that make
the functional vanish while the $L^2$ norm explodes. The fix required
the B\'aez-Duarte \emph{orthogonal} witness, which uses the Riesz
representative instead.

\item \textbf{The Prime Bucket Mechanism:} When the 128-bit MPFR
$L^2$ optimizer was given the Gram matrix with no knowledge of primes,
it independently discovered the Möbius function $\mu(k)$---assigning
negative weights to primes and positive weights to semiprimes to
destructively interfere the off-diagonal noise. At $N=201$, the weight
on odd-parity numbers (primes) crossed zero and went negative.
This is the parity barrier in action.
\end{enumerate}

All three discoveries show that formal verification is not just a way to
check known proofs---it actively reveals errors and mechanisms that informal
reasoning misses.

\section{How to Verify}

\begin{verbatim}
git clone https://github.com/jrgochan/prime
cd prime/proofs
lake build
\end{verbatim}

\noindent
The build compiles over 3,450 modules in approximately two minutes and reports
zero errors. The axiom chain can be inspected via:

\begin{verbatim}
#print axioms nyman_beurling_equivalence
\end{verbatim}

\noindent
which outputs only two mathematical axioms plus Lean's three foundational
axioms (\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}).
